These are the tags printed in the footnotes of the source advisory-opinion PDFs. Capitalization variants are consolidated in this directory, while each opinion page preserves the wording and capitalization used in that PDF.

This differs from [[topics/index|Topics]], which are broad, curated research categories applied across the whole corpus. Official opinion tags are narrower source metadata and apply only where an advisory opinion PDF supplies them.
